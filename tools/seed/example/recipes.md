# Example recipes (generic, safe to keep in the public repo)

## Chana dal lauki
- name_hindi: चना दाल लौकी
- category: dal
- dal_type: chana
- region: North Indian
- equipment: pressure cooker, kadai

### Ingredients
- 100 g chana dal (soak overnight)
- 350 g lauki; cubed
- 1 tomato
- 1/2 tsp turmeric

### Steps
1. The chana dal is already soaking — drain and rinse it first.
2. Pressure cook with lauki, turmeric and salt for 3 whistles.
3. Tadka of cumin, ginger and tomato; pour over and simmer.

## Barley vegetable soup
- category: soup
- soup_style: low-carb-veg
- equipment: pressure cooker

### Ingredients
- 30 g barley
- 2 carrot; diced

### Steps
1. Soak the barley in hot water for 30 minutes.
2. Pressure cook with vegetables for 3 whistles.

### Reheat
Reheat on low gas, add a splash of water if thick.
